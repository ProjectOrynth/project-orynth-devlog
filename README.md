## What is Project Orynth?

Project Orynth is an independent artificial-life experiment exploring the possibility of creating a persistent, autonomous ecosystem populated by an entirely fictional species.

The idea is simple:

Create a world. Introduce a small population of individuals. Give them the biological necessities, cognitive foundations and physical abilities to interact with their surroundings.

Then let them live.

There are no player-controlled characters, objectives, quests or predetermined progression. Orynth is not a game. It is intended to be a continuously running simulation, operating 24 hours a day.

Our role is to create the conditions for life, maintain the simulation and observe what happens.

## The Experiment

The experiment begins with five founding individuals, known as **Generation 001**, in an environment called **The Cradle**.

They will inhabit a world containing:

- A functioning natural ecosystem.
- Plants, animals and other living organisms.
- Finite natural resources.
- Changing weather and environmental conditions.
- Biological needs and survival requirements.
- The ability to explore, experiment and interact with physical objects.

The inhabitants will have individual memories, experiences, personalities and the capacity to learn.

We will not prescribe their culture, social structures, intelligence development or technological progression.

They may discover tools, agriculture, construction, fire, metallurgy or something entirely unexpected.

They may also fail to develop, struggle to survive or disappear entirely.

**No outcome is guaranteed.**

## Our Philosophy

We don't want to simulate a civilisation that has already been designed.

We want to create the conditions in which one might emerge.

The inhabitants will not receive instructions from us, and we will not intervene in their decisions.

Their successes, failures, discoveries and extinction are all valid outcomes of the experiment.

## Development Journal

This repository is the public record of Project Orynth.

Development entries will document:

- Design decisions and changes.
- Technical development and milestones.
- Challenges and experiments.
- Observations from the simulation.
- Unexpected discoveries and failures.

Entries will be published from the very beginning, including the earliest stages of development.

We believe the process is just as important as the eventual results.

## Current Status

**Phase:** Initial concept and design.

The project is currently in its planning stage. No simulation has been implemented yet.

The technical foundations, biological systems, ecosystem and cognitive architecture are still being developed.

## Follow the Experiment

This repository will remain publicly accessible throughout development.

The source code and technical implementation are maintained separately in a private repository.
